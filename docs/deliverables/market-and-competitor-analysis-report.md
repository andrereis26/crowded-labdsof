# Market and Competitor Analysis Report

## 1. Relevant Alternatives

### 1.1. Bandsintown

Bandsintown helps users discover concerts based on their location and musical preferences. It supports artist following, music-service connections, personalised recommendations and event alerts, directly overlapping with Crowded’s discovery features

### 1.2. Eventbrite

Eventbrite combines event discovery, registration and ticketing with organiser tools, including attendee communication and check-in. Its application also allows users to access tickets and connect with friends

### 1.3. Ticketline

Ticketline provides online and physical ticket sales, ticketing management and access-control services in Portugal. It also communicates cancellations and postponements to online buyers

### 1.4. Social Media

Platforms such as Facebook and WhatsApp help people share events, discuss plans and receive updates. Facebook supports event pages, while WhatsApp offers group communication, polls and event participation responses

## 2. Strengths and Weaknesses

| Alternatives | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| -------- | -------- | -------- |
| **Bandsintown** | Personalised concert discovery; music-service connections; configurable mobile search radius; artist following and alerts | Primarily focused on live music; ticket purchases can involve external services; structured event-specific crowd reporting was not established |
| **Eventbrite** | Discovery, registration and ticket access within one platform; organiser tools; attendee communication; friend connections | Documented ticket management centres on Eventbrite orders; external ticket import and structured live crowd reports were not established |
| **Ticketline** | Relevant Portuguese ticketing infrastructure; online and physical distribution; access control; cancellation and postponement communication | Operational access information does not necessarily provide public crowd conditions; attendee matching and cross-provider ticket storage were not established |
| **Social Media** | Existing audiences and relationships; group communication; event coordination; polls and rapid information sharing | Information can become fragmented across groups and pages; informal reports are difficult to aggregate and keep current; visibility and privacy vary by context |

## 3. Opportunity for differentiation 

### 3.1. Connected Event Experience

Crowded could allow users to discover an event, express interest, receive updates and check venue conditions through the same event page. Following an event would not require purchasing or uploading a ticket, making the platform useful across ticket providers and for free events

### 3.2. Trustworthy Information About Changes and Conditions

Crowded could combine official organiser announcements with recent attendee reports about crowding and entrance queues. Clear labels and timestamps would help users distinguish confirmed changes from community observations

### 3.3. Interest Polls That Support Planning

Organisers could use polls to assess interest in a proposed artist, date or location before finalising arrangements. Connecting responses to the relevant event and local audience could make them more useful for planning

Poll results should represent potential demand rather than guaranteed attendance. Their usefulness would need to be validated against later registrations and actual participation

### 3.4. Optional Connections Between Attendees

Crowded could help users find people with similar preferences or an interest in the same event. Connecting this functionality to a specific activity could make it easier to find company and start conversations

Participation should be optional, with control over attendance visibility and who can initiate contact
